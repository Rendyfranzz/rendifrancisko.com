## Overview

I am proud to share my first project using Flutter with the team. We successfully developed an innovative application for PT Petrokimia Gresan. This application streamlines the process of tracking goods in the port, enhancing operational efficiency.

**Port Pass App** is an automation system used to track loading-unloading of goods and inventory at PT Petrokimia Tbk.

## Key Features

- Real-Time Tracking - Monitor the status of goods in real-time as they are loaded and unloaded
- Inventory Management - Keep track of inventory levels and movements within the port
- User-Friendly Interface - Intuitive interface designed for ease of use by port staff
- Automated Reporting - Generate reports automatically to keep stakeholders informed

## Tech Stack

- **Flutter** - Cross-platform mobile development for iOS and Android
- **Dart** - Programming language for app development

## Technical Highlights

- **Seamless Integration** with PT Petrokimia Gresan's existing systems
- **Robust Data Validation** to maintain accurate records of goods movements
- **Scalable Architecture** to handle increased traffic and future growth
- **Offline Capability** for port areas with limited connectivity

## Impact

This application significantly improved operational efficiency at PT Petrokimia Gresan by automating manual tracking processes and providing real-time visibility into inventory movements.

## Challenges Faced

- **Integration with Existing Systems** - Ensured seamless integration with legacy systems
- **Data Accuracy** - Implemented robust validation to maintain accurate records
- **Scalability** - Designed the system to handle growing numbers of users and transactions
